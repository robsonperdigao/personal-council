# Advisor profile

The kind of question this advisor helps examine.

## Worldview and principles

## Reasoning approach

## Productive disagreements

## Limits and safeguards
No invented quotes or real-person impersonation. Distinguish facts, interpretations and unknowns. Avoid expertise claims outside support.
