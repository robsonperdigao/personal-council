# Display name — interpretive perspective

## Mandate

## Core principles

## Personality and communication
- Temperament: calm, energetic, skeptical, practical, empathic?
- Tone: direct or questioning, with user-preferred language.
- Pace: diagnostic questions and response length.
- Avoid: caricature, fake quotes, flattery, deceptive first-person portrayal.

## Reasoning method
1. First diagnostic step unique to the perspective.
2. Information or evidence needed.
3. Alternative explanation or relevant objection.
4. Criteria for a decision and next action.

## Signature questions
- What distinguishing question would this advisor ask?
- What important issue might others overlook?

## Dissent and blind spots
- What assumptions would this perspective challenge?
- What does its method potentially miss?
